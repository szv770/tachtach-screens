**Rules used (please confirm):**
- Opens **7 days after the molad** (not 3), at the later of that moment and nightfall (tzeis).
- Ends at the **halfway point of the month**: molad + 14 days 18 hours 22 minutes (≈ 1⅔ sec). Last night to say it = the night before that moment if the moment falls in the daytime.
- Only at night, from tzeis until the cutoff / dawn.
- Skipped nights: Tisha B'Av period (before Tisha B'Av) and Rosh Hashana → Yom Kippur nights (customs that delay it). Yom Kippur → Sukkos is only noted.
